D-03: Graph Identity Hash & Stale-Proposal Rejection Design
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In `explorer/src/lib/refactoring-simulation.ts`:
- `computeGraphHash(graphData)` (Lines 62–77):
  Computes a deterministic string representation from `schemaVersion`, `nodes.length`, `edges.length`, sorted `node.id`s, and sorted `edge.id`s, producing a 32-bit hex hash `gh-...`.
- `isProposalStale(proposal, currentProjectId, currentGraph)` (Lines 79–100):
  Validates `proposal.projectId === currentProjectId` AND `proposal.baseGraphHash === computeGraphHash(currentGraph)`.
- `validateProposalCommit(proposal, currentProjectId, currentGraph)` (Lines 102–111):
  Rejects stale proposals (`valid: false`) if the underlying graph hash has changed or project context mismatches, preventing stale or cross-project commits against modified graphs.
